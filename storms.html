Chapter Four

The Storms We Survived ⛈️

Every love story has sunshine.

But the ones that matter also have storms.

Ours was never perfect.

We were two people learning how to love while still learning ourselves.

There were moments when we misunderstood each other.

Moments when things felt heavier than they should have.

Moments when loving each other meant learning, growing, and trying again.

But somewhere along the way, we learned something important:

Love is not only found in the easy days.

It is found in the conversations after the silence.

The apologies.

The patience.

The choice to understand each other a little more than yesterday.

We are not the same two people who started this journey.

We have grown.

We have changed.

We have learned each other in ways we never expected.

And somehow, through every season...

I still find my way back to you.

Because even when the weather was rough,

you were still my favourite place; my home.
Forever and ever meri jaan.
