Chapter Five

One Day, Soon ✨

For so long, our love has existed between moments.

Waiting for the next call.
Counting down the days.
Missing the little things we never got to have.

But when I think about the future...

I don't think about anything complicated.

I think about home.

I think about waking up beside you.

I think about dancing around the kitchen while we make breakfast together.

I think about laughing at each other when we confidently sing the wrong lyrics.

I think about making your favourite meals and you making mine.

I think about road trips where enjoy our music and bask in how much we enjoy each others' company.

I think about the little fights where we both pretend we're annoyed...

but still end up needing our cuddle every night.

I think about drowning you in kermies until you tell me I've given enough.

(Which, realistically, will never happen.)

Maybe the future is not one huge moment.

Maybe it is thousands of tiny ones.

A million ordinary days that become extraordinary because they are with you: my best friend.

So until then, I'll keep waiting.

Because one day, soon...

I won't have to miss you from far away.

I'll just have to annoy you in person.
