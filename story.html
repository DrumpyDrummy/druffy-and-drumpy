Chapter One

When Sahu Met Zahu🌹

Every great love story has a beginning.

Ours started somewhere unexpected: Instagram.

Just a boy, a girl, and a conversation about support and music.

Except somehow, that conversation became the start of everything.

At first, you were just Moon.

A boy who was mysterious, ridiculously smooth, annoyingly charming, and somehow always knew what to say.

(Honestly, a suspicious amount of confidence for one person.)

But somewhere between the jokes, the late-night conversations, and getting to know you...

you became Sahu.

and I became Zahu.

And somehow, we became us.

I didn't know that a random Instagram conversation would become my favourite notification.

I didn't know that I would wait weeks for you to ask for my number.

I didn't know that not talking to you would make me miss you.

I didn't know I was meeting someone who would become my home.

I just knew I liked talking to you.

The rest came after. ❤️
