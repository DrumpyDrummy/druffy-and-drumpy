☁️ Where We Meet In Dreams

A place only we know

When the world goes quiet
and the stars begin to glow,
there is a place where we find each other,
somewhere only our hearts know.

No miles between us,
no clocks to count the days,
just you and me floating softly
through endless skies of blue and gold rays.

We drift through clouds like oceans,
laughing among the stars,
forgetting for a moment
how far away we are.

Then hand in hand we wander
through a forest made of light,
where glowing trees surround us
and everything feels right.

We run through fields of magic,
where flowers bloom beneath our feet,
chasing little sparks of wonder
down a path where dreams and reality meet.

And when morning finally finds us,
and the dream begins to fade,
I hope somewhere inside your heart
you remember the world we made.

Because even when we wake up
and return to where we are,
You'll always find me waiting
somewhere beyond the stars.
