🚗 The Road Trip We Haven't Taken Yet

A map full of places we haven't found

One day we'll pick a road
with no perfect plan,
just a destination written somewhere
on a piece of paper in our hands.

We'll probably miss a turn,
take the wrong way twice,
argue with the map dramatically
then call the mistake "nice".

We'll find little hidden places
that nobody knows exist,
tiny towns and random stops
we never planned to visit.

We'll collect strange memories
from places without a name,
stories that sound ridiculous
but we'll remember all the same.

We'll take too many pictures,
save things we'll never need,
Stop at random places just to kiss
And then at bookstores to get me books to read.

And maybe years from now
we won't remember where we went,
but we'll remember looking over
and knowing exactly what it meant.

Because the best adventures
are not always where you arrive,
sometimes they're just two people
making ordinary roads come alive. I love you so incredibly much baby.
