🕵️ The Anniversary Investigation

Official Report of a Very Suspicious Incident. In poetism.

On a certain special day,
A mystery came to light,
A certain someone forgot something,
And claimed they were alright.

The evidence was gathered,
The clues were clearly shown,
Two anniversaries were missing,
And the suspect had known.

The suspect said:
"How could I forget?"

The investigator replied:
"Brother, we have the evidence yet."

A memory test was conducted,
A dramatic trial began,
The jury looked around and said:
"Sahu better not do it again."

Final judgement was reached,
The verdict was finally true:

Guilty of forgetting our first two anniversaries,
But still completely loved by Zahu.
