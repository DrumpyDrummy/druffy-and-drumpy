Chapter Two

Two Souls Across the World 🌍

Some love stories begin with two people in the same place.

Ours began with two people who were always a little too far away.

One of us waking up while the other was ending the day.

Two countries.
Two time zones.
Two different worlds.

And somehow, one person I still wanted to tell everything to.

Distance meant missing the little things.

The hugs we couldn't have.
The moments we couldn't share.
The ordinary days we had to experience separately.

But somehow, calls became dates.
Messages became memories.
And a screen became the place where we found each other every day.

You taught me that love is not only about being beside someone.

Sometimes, it is choosing someone even when they are miles away.

It is waiting.
It is patience.
It is saying “I wish you were here” and still finding reasons to smile. It is missing you even when I'm in a room full of people.

And one day, the distance will just be a story we tell.

A reminder that even when the world was huge...

we still found our way back to each other.
